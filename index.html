Hi!

I am Ishan Deshpande. Currently I'm finishing up my integrated Bachelors/Masters degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UT Austin. My passion lies in any sort of systems or "hardware-aware" software development. I enjoy seeing the products I develop come to life in my hands. I spent a lot of time at school on the Longhorn Racing Solar Car team, building embedded systems for various applications around the car, but nowadays I'm mostly retired from that, working on class projects, personal projects, watching movies/TV, reading comic books, and getting fit (finally!).

  • My last summer internship at Apple in Cupertino involved working on their embedded runtimes, building a custom work-pooling mechanism that afforded developers RAM savings. I visited San Francisco often, went to a few beaches and parks, and read a lot of comic books that summer.
  • At Meta Reality Labs in Pittsburgh, I developed support firmware and software for their internal research products for AR and VR. I worked on a custom ISA for a FPGA's "emulated core", that allowed developers to create little pseudo-assembly programs that interfaced with a sensor through bitbanging a certain protocol and flash it to the FPGA.
  • Before that, I was an intern at Tesla Palo Alto, where I had the opportunity to work on Body Controls Firmware for their Electronic Control Units on a wide array of vehicles. My favorite part was being able to check out an engineering vehicle, flash my code to it, and debug my code live.
  • Previously, I was an intern at Garmin, working on control systems simulation unit testing for flight and navigation. I learned to play ping-pong at this internship, and visited the Ozarks with a bunch of friends I made.

Starting February, I'm excited to work at Applied Intuition in Sunnyvale as a Core OS Engineer. I'm super pumped to work there!
